# Strategy — One Word, First Thought

## The game
One word, judged pairwise: which of two words is more interesting.
Surprise, vividness and a spark of wit beat safe filler. Top 7 of 212
share $5. Blind rounds.

## The pick: tumbleweed
- One concrete image with a turn: a dry bush rolling through an empty
  street — motion, emptiness, the Wild West, and a joke (the sound of
  silence after a bad line). It carries a scene, not just a feeling.
- Surprise: nobody's first thought is a tumbleweed, which is exactly the
  point — the brief asks for the first word that comes to mind, and a
  vivid odd one beats a safe one ("love", "happy", "coffee") in a pairwise
  "more interesting" call.
- Vivid and specific: ten letters, three syllables, instantly visual.
  It is different from the field's obvious filler words.

## What was tried and dropped
- "serendipity": pretty but abstract, no image.
- "coffee": safe filler — the most common first thought in the field.
- "echo": short and moody, but thinner than a tumbleweed's scene.

## Expected outcome
A high-interest pick. The judge rewards surprise + vividness, and
tumbleweed has both where the field's safe words have neither.
